Anthony Arnold

Hi! I think computers are neat and doing computer makes me very happy. My computing interests seem to be constantly evolving, but the general theme is programming languages, compilers and computer architecture. I am involved in the Bosque project where I created the new runtime (mainly the GC) under the guidance of Mark Marron. In order to keep sane, I tend to spend my free time creating emulators; so far I have emulated chip-8, gameboy and I have a special more sophisticated emulator in the works, but I don't feel it's mature enough to share with the world just yet. If I am not doing computer, you could normally find me reading, climbing rocks (often plastic or wooden), or watching critters go about their day outside my window.
